Certificate eligibility

How the final score works

Final score = assignment score + exam score. You must meet both minimum eligibility criteria, even if your combined score is 40/100 or higher.

25%

Assignment score

Based on the average of your best 8 assignments out of 12. This component contributes up to 25 marks. Assignments include quizzes, programming tasks and essay submissions for the week.

Minimum required: 10 / 25
75%

Proctored exam score

Your certification exam score out of 100 is weighted to contribute up to 75 marks to the final score.

Minimum required: 30 / 75

Both minimums are compulsory. You are eligible for a certificate only if your assignment score is at least 10/25 and your exam component is at least 30/75. Missing either threshold means no certificate, regardless of the combined total.

Certificate notes

What you receive

Certificate details

The certificate includes your name, photograph and final exam score with its breakup, along with NPTEL and IIT Kharagpur logos.

Digital certificate only

Only the e-certificate is issued. Hard copies are not dispatched. Verify certificates at nptel.ac.in/noc ↗.

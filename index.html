Certificate criteria

How your final score is calculated.

Your final score combines the assignment component and the proctored exam. Both minimum eligibility rules must be met.

100 marks total
Assignment score contributes 25/100 Exam score contributes 75/100
25%

Assignment score

Based on the average of your best 8 assignments out of the 12 assignments in the course, weighted to a maximum of 25 marks. Assignments include all types available that week, such as quizzes, programming tasks and essay submissions.

Minimum eligibility: 10 out of 25
75%

Proctored exam score

Your in-person certification exam score out of 100 is weighted to a maximum of 75 marks in the final score.

Minimum eligibility: 30 out of 75
Important: both minimums are compulsory.

You are eligible for a certificate only if your assignment score is at least 10/25 and your exam score is at least 30/75. A final score of 40/100 or more does not qualify you if either minimum is missed.
